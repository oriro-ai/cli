# Australian and New Zealand Education

## Australian education system

### Year 11-12 and ATAR

ATAR (Australian Tertiary Admission Rank): 0-99.95 percentile rank among Year 12 completers.
Each state has own curriculum and assessment system.

**NSW:** HSC (Higher School Certificate). ATAR calculated from HSC marks scaled to rank.
**Victoria:** VCE (Victorian Certificate of Education). Study scores 0-50 per subject.
**Queensland:** No external final exams since 2020. School-based assessment + external.
**Western Australia:** WACE (Western Australian Certificate of Education).

**ATAR targets:**
Medicine: 99.00+ at most universities.
Law: 96.00+.
Engineering: 85.00+.
Most undergraduate degrees: 60.00-80.00.

### University structure

G8 (Group of Eight): Melbourne, Sydney, ANU, Queensland, Monash, Adelaide, UNSW, UWA.
3-year bachelor's degrees standard (some 4 years with honours).
**Direct entry from school** common. No SAT/ACT equivalent.

## New Zealand education — NCEA

NCEA (National Certificate of Educational Achievement): Levels 1, 2, 3.
Modular: Standards achieved (A), Merit (M), Excellence (E).
University Entrance: 14 Level 3 credits in 3 approved subjects + literacy + numeracy.

**University ranking NZ:**
Auckland (ranked #1 NZ, top 100 globally), Otago, Victoria Wellington, Canterbury.
