# Education Sector Industry

## EdTech market

Global EdTech market ~$340B (2024). Growing 15%+ annually.
**Major segments:** K-12 digital learning, higher education platforms, corporate training, language learning, test prep.
**Key players:** Coursera, Udemy, Duolingo, Chegg, 2U, Kahoot, Canvas/Instructure.

## Learning Management Systems (LMS)

**Canvas:** Most used in US higher education. Clean UI. API-friendly.
**Moodle:** Open source. Highly customizable. Used by 250M users globally.
**Google Classroom:** Free. Deep Google Workspace integration. K-12 dominant.
**Blackboard/Anthology:** Legacy enterprise. Large HE installations.

## Education business models

**B2C:** Student pays directly. Duolingo (freemium), Masterclass (subscription).
**B2B:** Institution pays for all students. Canvas, Blackboard.
**B2B2C:** Employer pays for employee learning. Coursera for Business, Udemy Business.
**Bootcamp model:** Intensive short course. $10K-$20K. Income Share Agreement option.

## Higher education funding models

**US:** Tuition + endowment returns + research grants + government funding.
**UK:** £9,250/year tuition cap. Government backed student loans.
**Germany/Norway:** Largely free public universities. Government funded.
**Australia:** HECS-HELP income-contingent loans. Repay when earning above threshold.

## PISA (Programme for International Student Assessment)

OECD test of 15-year-olds every 3 years. Reading, Mathematics, Science.
**Top performers (2022):** Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Estonia, Ireland.
**US performance:** Average — 26th in reading, 34th in mathematics.
**India:** Does not participate in PISA.
