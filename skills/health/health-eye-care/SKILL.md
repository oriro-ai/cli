# Eye Care and Vision Health

## Common refractive errors

**Myopia (nearsightedness):** Objects far away appear blurry. Eyeball too long.
Prevalence: 30% globally. Up to 90% in some East Asian populations aged 20.
**Hyperopia (farsightedness):** Objects close appear blurry. Eyeball too short.
**Astigmatism:** Irregular cornea curvature. Blurry at all distances.
**Presbyopia:** Age-related loss of near focus. Begins ~age 40. Reading glasses needed.

## Prescription notation

OD (Oculus Dexter) = right eye. OS (Oculus Sinister) = left eye.
**Sphere (SPH):** Refractive correction. Negative = myopia. Positive = hyperopia.
**Cylinder (CYL):** Astigmatism correction.
**Axis:** Orientation of astigmatism (1-180°).
Example: -3.00 -1.25 × 090 = myopic with astigmatism at 90°.

## Vision correction options

**Spectacles:** Safest. No complications. Style and prescription combined.
**Soft contact lenses:** Daily disposable safest (no cleaning required).
**Orthokeratology:** Night-worn rigid lenses that temporarily reshape cornea. Myopia control.
**LASIK:** Laser reshapes cornea permanently. Best for stable myopia -1 to -8D.
**PRK:** Laser on surface. Better for thin corneas than LASIK.
**ICL:** Implantable lens. For high myopia beyond LASIK range.

## Eye diseases

**Glaucoma:** Optic nerve damage. Often elevated eye pressure. Silent thief of sight.
Annual IOP (intraocular pressure) check important after age 40.
**Macular degeneration (AMD):** Central vision loss. Dry (90%) and wet forms.
Risk factors: Age, smoking, UV exposure. Lutein/zeaxanthin diet protective.
**Diabetic retinopathy:** Leading cause of blindness in working-age adults.
Annual dilated eye exam mandatory for diabetics.
**Cataracts:** Lens clouding. Common after age 60. Surgery highly effective.

## Eye health habits

20-20-20 rule: Every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
Outdoor time: 2+ hours/day protective against myopia progression in children.
UV protection: Sunglasses with UV400 or 100% UVA/UVB protection.
