# Chartered Professional Examinations

## CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst)

**Three levels:** Sequential. Pass each to proceed.
**Pass rates:** Level 1 ~35-45%, Level 2 ~40-45%, Level 3 ~50-55%.
**Experience required:** 4,000 hours relevant work for charter.

### CFA Level 1 topic weights

Ethics and Professional Standards: 15-20% (CRITICAL — tested all 3 levels).
Financial Statement Analysis: 11-14%.
Equity Investments: 10-12%.
Fixed Income: 10-12%.
Portfolio Management: 5-8%.

### Study approach

**Study hours:** Minimum 300 hours per level.
**Resources:** CFA Institute curriculum (included with registration) + Schweser notes.
**Free:** CFA Institute practice questions + mock exams included with registration.
Ethics readings: Read multiple times. Critical for all three levels.

### CFA Level 2 — Equity valuation emphasis

Item set format: 88 MCQs in vignette format. Application of financial models.

### CFA Level 3 — Portfolio management

Constructed response (essay) + item sets.
Institutional and individual portfolio management, asset allocation, risk management.

## CA (Chartered Accountant) — India (ICAI)

**Three stages:** Foundation → Intermediate → Final.
**Articleship:** 3 years practical training (concurrent with Intermediate/Final).

**CA Final (6 papers):**
Group 1: Financial Reporting, Strategic Financial Management, Advanced Auditing.
Group 2: Corporate Laws, Strategic Cost Management, Direct Tax Laws.
**Difficulty:** 5-8% pass rates for both groups in some attempts.

## ACCA (UK/Global)

**13 exams + 3 years experience + Ethics module.**
Applied Knowledge → Applied Skills → Strategic Professional.
Exemptions available for recognized degree holders.

## FRM (Financial Risk Manager) — GARP

**Two exams:** FRM Part 1 (100 MCQ) → FRM Part 2 (80 MCQ).
Part 1: Foundations of Risk, Quantitative Analysis, Financial Markets, Valuation.
Part 2: Market Risk, Credit Risk, Operational Risk, Investment Management.
**Pass rates:** Part 1 ~45%, Part 2 ~55%.
