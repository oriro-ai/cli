# Spanish and Latin American Education

## Spain — Education system

### Bachillerato (ages 16-18)

2-year pre-university course. 3 tracks: Science/Technology, Humanities/Social, Arts.
**EVAU (Evaluación de Bachillerato para el Acceso a la Universidad):** University entrance exam.
Subjects tested: Spanish Language, Spanish History, Foreign Language, plus Bachillerato track subjects.
Grade: 0-10. Admission score = 60% Bachillerato average + 40% EVAU.
Top scores: Medicine (12+/14), Architecture (11+), Engineering (10+).

### Spanish universities

Universidad de Barcelona, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, Universidad de Salamanca (oldest, 1218).
Polytechnic University of Madrid (UPM), UPC Barcelona: Top engineering.

## Latin America

### Mexico

**EXANI-II (CENEVAL):** National university entrance exam used by many universities.
UNAM: Most competitive. UNAM entrance exam required. 10% acceptance rate.
IPN, Tec de Monterrey, UANL.

### Colombia

**Saber 11 (ICFES):** National standardized test for university admissions. 0-500 scale.
Universidad de los Andes, Universidad Nacional (public, free), Pontificia Universidad Javeriana.

### Brazil (separate — Portuguese)

ENEM: Key for Brazilian higher education admissions.

### Argentina

UBA (Universidad de Buenos Aires): Free, public, prestigious. World-ranked.
Admission: CBC (Ciclo Básico Común) — 1 year common cycle first.

### Chile

PDT (Prueba de Transición): University selection test. Replaces PSU since 2021.
PUC Chile, Universidad de Chile: Top ranked in Latin America.

Sources: CENEVAL Mexico (free), ICFES Colombia (free), Spanish Ministry of Education (free)
