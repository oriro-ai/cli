# Business Writing

## Core principles

**Clarity:** One idea per sentence. Short sentences (average 15-20 words).
**Brevity:** Cut every unnecessary word. "Due to the fact that" → "because."
**Active voice:** "The team completed the project" not "The project was completed by the team."
**Specific over vague:** "Revenue increased 23% in Q3" not "Revenue improved significantly."

## Email structure

**Subject line:** Specific and actionable. "Action needed: Q3 budget approval by Friday" not "Budget."
**Opening:** State purpose in first sentence. Skip "I hope this email finds you well."
**Body:** One topic per email. Three sentences max per paragraph.
**Closing:** Clear next action. Who does what by when.

```
Subject: Decision needed: vendor contract renewal — respond by Dec 15

Hi Sarah,

I need your approval to renew our contract with Acme Corp for 2024.

The contract (attached) runs $45,000/year — same terms as last year.
If approved, I'll sign and return by December 20 to lock in our rate.

Please reply with your approval or any questions.

Best,
Marcus
```

## Report structure

**Executive summary:** Written last. One page maximum. Decision-makers read only this.
Key elements: Problem → Method → Findings → Recommendation.
**Body:** Data and analysis supporting executive summary.
**Appendices:** Detailed data, methodology, raw numbers.

## Proposals

**Problem statement:** What pain does the client have? How big is it?
**Proposed solution:** What specifically will you do?
**Evidence:** Why will it work? Case studies, data.
**Investment:** Cost, timeline, deliverables.
**Call to action:** Clear next step.

## Plain language rules

Use common words: "use" not "utilize," "start" not "commence," "help" not "facilitate."
Define acronyms on first use.
Use headers, bullets, and numbered lists for scanability.
Test with target reader before finalizing.
