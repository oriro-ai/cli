# Indonesian and Malaysian Education

## Indonesia

### Education structure

SD (Sekolah Dasar, Primary) 6 years → SMP (Junior High) 3 years → SMA/SMK (Senior High/Vocational) 3 years → University.

### University admissions — SNBP, SNBT, Mandiri

**SNBP (Seleksi Nasional Berdasarkan Prestasi):** Academic achievement-based selection. No exam.
**SNBT (Seleksi Nasional Berdasarkan Tes):** National entrance test — UTBKTest.
**Mandiri:** Independent university-run exam.
Top universities: UI (Universitas Indonesia), ITB (Institut Teknologi Bandung), UGM (Universitas Gadjah Mada).

### Bahasa Indonesia

Official language. Simple grammar — no tenses, gender, or conjugation.
**UKBI (Uji Kemahiran Berbahasa Indonesia):** Official proficiency test for foreigners.

## Malaysia

### Education structure

Primary (KBSR, 6 years) → Secondary Form 1-5 → STPM Form 6 (pre-university) or Matriculation.

### SPM (Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia)

Malaysian equivalent of O-Level/GCSE. Taken at Form 5 (age 17).
11+ subjects. Grades A+ to G. Results used for post-secondary admissions.

### STPM (Sijil Tinggi Pelajaran Malaysia)

Pre-university. Malaysian A-Level equivalent. Highly rigorous. CGPA-based.
Alternative: Matriculation Programme (1 year, quota-based, easier path to university).

### Top universities Malaysia

University of Malaya (UM) — #1 Malaysia, QS Top 200.
UTM, UPM, UKM, UTAR (private).

### Bahasa Malaysia

Official language. Mutually intelligible with Bahasa Indonesia (different vocabulary ~30%).
**MPLC (Malaysian Proficiency Level Certificate):** Language proficiency certification.

Sources: Kemdikbud Indonesia (kemdikbud.go.id — free), MOE Malaysia (moe.gov.my — free)
