# German Education System

## School structure

Grundschule (Primary, 4-6 years) → Secondary split:
**Gymnasium:** Academic track. Leads to Abitur. University preparation.
**Realschule:** Practical track.
**Hauptschule:** Vocational track.
**Gesamtschule:** Comprehensive school with all tracks.

## Abitur

Final exam after 12-13 years of schooling (varies by state).
Grades: 1.0 (best) to 4.0 (minimum pass). 1.0-1.5 = excellent.
**Abitur average (Numerus Clausus - NC):** Many university programs require minimum Abitur grade.
Medicine: NC ~1.0-1.2. Law: ~1.5. Engineering: 2.0-2.5.

## German universities

Germany: Tuition-free at public universities (just ~€300 semester administrative fee).
**TU9 Alliance:** 9 leading technical universities: TU Munich, RWTH Aachen, TU Berlin, KIT, etc.
**Excellence Initiative:** Elite cluster research universities.
LMU Munich and TU Munich ranked globally.

## Application

**uni-assist:** Central application service for international students.
**DAAD:** German Academic Exchange Service. Scholarships for international students.
TestDaF or DSH: German language proficiency required. B2/C1 level minimum.

## Vocational education (Berufsausbildung)

Dual system: Learn in company + attend Berufsschule 2 days/week.
Highly respected. 50%+ of Germans complete vocational training.

Sources: DAAD (daad.de — free), Hochschulstart (hochschulstart.de — free)
