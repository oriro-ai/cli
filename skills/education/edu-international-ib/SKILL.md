# International Baccalaureate (IB)

## IB Diploma Programme (IBDP)

Taken at ages 16-19 globally. Recognized by universities worldwide.
**6 subject groups:** Studies in Language and Literature, Language Acquisition,
Individuals and Societies, Sciences, Mathematics, The Arts.

Choose 3 subjects at Higher Level (HL), 3 at Standard Level (SL).

**Core components (mandatory):**
Theory of Knowledge (TOK): 1600-word essay + 10-minute presentation. Explores nature of knowledge.
Extended Essay (EE): 4,000-word independent research essay in chosen subject.
CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service): Extracurricular portfolio.

### Scoring

Each subject: 1-7 points. TOK + EE: 0-3 bonus points.
Maximum: 45 points (42 subjects + 3 bonus).
Score 24 = minimum pass. Score 38+ = competitive for top universities.

### Popular HL subjects

**HL Mathematics Analysis and Approaches:** Rigorous calculus. For STEM.
**HL Physics:** University-level intro. Calculus integrated.
**HL Economics:** Micro, macro, international, development. Lots of commentary writing.
**HL English Literature:** 20th century literature. Literary commentary.

## HL vs SL distinction

HL: More depth, more content, more demanding assessments.
SL: Broader coverage, less depth.
UK Russell Group: Often require 6+ in relevant HL subjects.

## University recognition

**UK:** 38+ points competitive for top Russell Group. Medicine requires 40+.
**US:** Scores 5-7 may earn college credit. Strong universities value IB rigor.
**Canada/Australia:** Widely recognized. Specific point requirements per university.
**India:** Accepted for Indian universities but JEE/NEET still required for engineering/medicine.
