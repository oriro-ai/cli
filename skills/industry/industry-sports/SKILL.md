# Sports Management

## Sports business economics

### Revenue streams (professional sports)

**Media rights:** Often 50-70% of league revenue. NFL: ~$110B+ in TV deals. NBA, MLB, NHL: Significant TV/streaming rights.
**Ticket sales:** Gate receipts. Suite/premium seating is disproportionately valuable.
**Sponsorships:** Jersey patches, naming rights (stadium), presenting sponsors, official partners.
**Merchandise/licensing:** Team-branded products. League-controlled licensing.
**Venue:** Concerts, events when team not playing.
**Expansion/franchise fees:** New teams paying into the league.

### Player contracts and salary caps

**Salary cap:** Maximum team payroll. Hard cap (NHL, NFL) vs. luxury tax soft cap (NBA, MLB).
**Guaranteed vs. non-guaranteed:** NFL contracts mostly non-guaranteed; NBA mostly guaranteed.
**Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA):** Agreement between league and players' union governing all terms. Governs revenue split, salary caps, free agency, arbitration, discipline.
**Arbitration (MLB):** Salary determined by arbitrator based on comparable players. Players typically eligible after 3 years.
**Free agency:** After CBA-determined service time, can negotiate with any team.

## Athlete representation

### Agent's role

Negotiate contracts, endorsements, appearance fees. Manage career.
NFLPA/NBPA/MLBPA: Player associations certify agents.
**Standard commission:** 3-5% of contracts, 10-20% of endorsements.
**Fiduciary duty:** Agent must act in client's best interest. Conflicts of interest strictly regulated.

### Sports science

**Performance optimization:** Sports science staff (strength/conditioning, sports medicine, analytics).
**Load management:** Monitoring training and competitive workload to prevent injury. GPS tracking, heart rate variability, sleep monitoring.
**Biomechanics:** Video analysis of movement mechanics. Injury prevention and technique optimization.
**Sports psychology:** Mental performance coaching. Visualization, focus techniques, pressure management.

## Venue operations

**Facility management:** Building systems, security, event operations.
**Revenue maximization:** Concert bookings, private events, suites/hospitality packages.
**Fan experience:** Food and beverage, technology (wifi, cashless payments, mobile ordering), wayfinding.
**Sustainability:** LEED certification for venues, food waste reduction, renewable energy.

## Sports media

**Broadcast rights:** Local (regional sports networks) + national (network TV, streaming).
**Cord-cutting impact:** RSN (Regional Sports Network) model under stress. Direct-to-consumer streaming migration.
**Social media:** Teams generate significant content. Fan engagement platforms.
