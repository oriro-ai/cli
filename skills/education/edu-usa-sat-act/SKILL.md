# SAT, ACT, and AP Exams

## Digital SAT (2024 format)

**Format:** 2 modules, adaptive.
Reading and Writing: 54 questions, 64 minutes.
Math: 44 questions, 70 minutes.
Total: 2 hours 14 minutes. Score: 400-1600.

**SAT Reading and Writing:**
Command of Evidence, Information and Ideas, Craft and Structure,
Standard English Conventions (punctuation, grammar, sentence structure).

**SAT Math:**
Algebra (35%), Advanced Math (35%), Problem-Solving/Data Analysis (15%),
Geometry and Trigonometry (15%). Calculator allowed throughout.

**Score targets:**
1400+ = competitive for most universities.
1500+ = competitive for selective schools.
1550+ = competitive for Ivy League.
**Free prep:** Khan Academy Official SAT Prep — full free program.

## ACT

**4 sections:** English (45 min, 75 questions), Mathematics (60 min, 60 questions),
Reading (35 min, 40 questions), Science (35 min, 40 questions).
Score: 1-36 composite. Average ~20. 30+ = top 5%.
Science section tests data interpretation, not specific science knowledge.

**SAT vs ACT choice:**
SAT: Better for students strong in algebra and evidence-based reading.
ACT: Better for students comfortable with time pressure.
Take both practice tests to see which suits you.

## AP (Advanced Placement) Exams

38+ subjects. Score 1-5. Score 3+ = college credit at many universities.
**Most taken:** AP Calculus AB/BC, AP English Language, AP Statistics,
AP Biology, AP Chemistry, AP Physics, AP US History, AP Computer Science A.
**Free prep:** College Board AP Classroom, Khan Academy (most subjects).
