# East Indian Education Systems

## West Bengal

### WBJEE (West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination)

For engineering and technology admissions in West Bengal.
**Subjects:** Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry. 200 questions, 200 marks.
**Rank calculation:** Purely WBJEE rank (no board marks weightage unlike some states).
Top colleges: Jadavpur University, IIEST Shibpur, NIT Durgapur, Presidency University.

### West Bengal Board (WBCHSE)

HS (Higher Secondary) examination. Unique optional subjects including Sanskrit, Philosophy.
Kolkata-centric strong humanities and science culture.

## Jharkhand

### JAC (Jharkhand Academic Council)

Class 10 (Matric) and Class 12 (Intermediate) examinations.
**Engineering admissions:** JEE Main for NITs + JCECE (Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Exam).
Top institution: NIT Jamshedpur, BIT Mesra Ranchi.

## Odisha

### OJEE (Odisha Joint Entrance Examination)

For lateral entry (3rd year) engineering. Also pharmacy and MBA.
Direct 1st year engineering: JEE Main + board marks.
Top institutions: NIT Rourkela (top in East India for engineering), ITER Bhubaneswar.

## Bihar

### BCECE (Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination)

Engineering and medical admissions in Bihar.
**Engineering:** PCM. Medical: PCB.
Top institutions: NIT Patna, BIT Patna.
Significant JEE coaching culture — Kota equivalent: Super 30 Patna legacy.

## Common elements across East India

Strong JEE culture particularly in Bihar (Super 30 foundation).
NEET for medical across all states — mandatory.
Board exams (CBSE, ISC, state boards) all accepted.
Railway recruitment boards heavily utilized in region — competitive government jobs.
