# Skin Health and Dermatology

## Skin basics

**Three layers:** Epidermis (outer, barrier), Dermis (collagen, blood vessels, hair follicles, glands), Subcutis/Hypodermis (fat, insulation).
**Skin functions:** Barrier (infection, UV), temperature regulation, sensation, vitamin D synthesis.
**Skin types:** Normal, dry, oily, combination, sensitive. Can change with age, climate, products.

## Sun protection (most important anti-aging and cancer prevention)

**UV radiation:** UVA (ages skin, penetrates glass) + UVB (burns, main cause of skin cancer).
**SPF:** Measures UVB protection only. SPF 30 blocks 97%. SPF 50 blocks 98%. Diminishing returns above 50.
**Broad spectrum:** Protects against both UVA and UVB. Required on the label.
**Application:** 1 ounce (shot glass) for full body. Reapply every 2 hours in sun, after swimming/sweating.
**Daily use:** On face even without direct sun exposure. UVA penetrates windows.
**Melanoma:** Most dangerous skin cancer. ABCDEs: Asymmetry, Border irregularity, multiple Colors, Diameter > 6mm, Evolving. Any changing mole → dermatologist.

## Common skin conditions

### Acne

**Pathophysiology:** Excess sebum + C. acnes bacteria + clogged pores + inflammation.
**Treatment ladder:**
Mild: Benzoyl peroxide (kills bacteria) or salicylic acid (exfoliates) — OTC.
Moderate: Topical retinoids (tretinoin) + topical antibiotics — prescription.
Severe: Oral antibiotics + topical retinoids, or isotretinoin (Accutane) for severe nodular/cystic.
**Retinoids:** Most evidence for acne AND anti-aging. Start slowly (every other night), expect initial irritation. Results in 3-6 months.

### Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis)

Chronic inflammatory skin condition. Itchy, red, dry patches. Often begins in childhood.
**Management:** Moisturize frequently (thick creams, not lotions). Avoid triggers (soaps, certain fabrics, temperature extremes). Topical corticosteroids for flares. Dupilumab (Dupixent) biologic for moderate-severe.

### Psoriasis

Autoimmune. Rapid skin cell turnover → thick plaques with silvery scales. Elbows, knees, scalp common.
Can be associated with psoriatic arthritis.
Treatment: Topical corticosteroids, vitamin D analogues, systemic biologics for severe (TNF inhibitors, IL-17, IL-23 inhibitors).

## Skincare routine (evidence-based)

**Morning:**

1. Gentle cleanser (if needed — many people only need water in AM)
2. Vitamin C serum (antioxidant, brightening)
3. Moisturizer
4. SPF 30+ broad spectrum

**Evening:**

1. Cleanser (double-cleanse if wore SPF)
2. Retinol/retinoid (anti-aging, acne)
3. Moisturizer

**Don't need:** Most of what advertising says you need. Fewer products with proven ingredients beat elaborate routines with trendy ones.
Proven ingredients: SPF (prevent), Retinoids (treat aging, acne), Vitamin C (antioxidant), Niacinamide (multiple benefits), Hyaluronic acid (hydration), Ceramides (barrier repair).
