# Customer Success Management

## Core philosophy

Customer success ≠ customer support. Support = reactive. CS = proactive.
Goal: Ensure customers achieve their desired outcomes using your product.
**CS drives NRR (Net Revenue Retention):** Revenue from existing customers after churn + expansion.
NRR > 100% = expansion exceeds churn. Best-in-class SaaS: NRR 120%+.

## Customer health scoring

**Leading indicators:** Login frequency, feature adoption, support tickets, NPS.
**Health score:** Weighted composite. Green (healthy), Yellow (at-risk), Red (critical).
Alert at yellow. Intervene at red. Don't wait until churn notice.

## Onboarding

Goal: Time-to-value (TTV). First 90 days critical for retention.
**Kickoff call:** Agree success criteria, timeline, roles and responsibilities.
**Milestone tracking:** First login, first key feature use, first measurable outcome.
Good onboarding = 70%+ feature adoption in 30 days.

## QBR (Quarterly Business Review)

Recurring strategic review with customer. Not a product demo.
**Agenda:** Business outcomes review → product utilization → roadmap preview → renewal/expansion.
**QBR should demonstrate ROI** in customer's terms, not vendor metrics.

## Churn signals

**Active churn:** Customer tells you they're leaving.
**Passive churn:** Customer stops engaging but doesn't cancel (for free tiers).
**Warning signals:** Declining logins, decreased data volume, unresponsive contacts, support escalations, champion leaving company.

## CS metrics

**Churn rate:** % customers lost. Monthly or annual. SaaS target: <5% annual.
**NPS:** Net Promoter Score. Survey: 0-10 likelihood to recommend.
**CSAT:** Post-interaction satisfaction. Immediate feedback.
**Time-to-value:** Days from contract signed to customer achieving first key outcome.
