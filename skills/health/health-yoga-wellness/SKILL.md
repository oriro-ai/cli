# Yoga and Wellness

## Yoga traditions

**Hatha Yoga:** Physical postures (asana) + breathing (pranayama). Foundation of most Western yoga.
**Vinyasa/Flow:** Postures linked with breath in continuous movement.
**Ashtanga:** Fixed sequence, breath-movement synchronization. Physically demanding.
**Iyengar:** Precision alignment, props (blocks, straps, bolsters). Therapeutic.
**Yin Yoga:** Long holds (3-5 minutes), targeting connective tissue.
**Restorative:** Fully supported postures. Deep relaxation.

## Key asanas

**Tadasana (Mountain Pose):** Foundation. Feet hip-width, weight even, lengthen spine.
**Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward Dog):** Inverted V. Press floor, lift hips, lengthen spine.
**Virabhadrasana I (Warrior 1):** Front knee 90°, back leg straight, arms up.
**Trikonasana (Triangle):** Wide stance, torso hinges sideways, bottom hand to shin.
**Bhujangasana (Cobra):** Prone, lift chest with back muscles.
**Ustrasana (Camel):** Kneeling, reach back for heels, open chest.

## Pranayama (breath practices)

**Ujjayi:** Slight throat constriction. Ocean sound. Used in Vinyasa.
**Nadi Shodhana (Alternate Nostril):** Right thumb closes right nostril, inhale left, etc. Balancing.
**Kapalabhati:** Rapid forceful exhales. Energizing. Contraindicated in pregnancy, high BP.
**Box breathing:** Equal inhale:hold:exhale:hold (4:4:4:4). Stress regulation.

## Patanjali's 8 limbs of yoga

1. **Yama:** Ethical restraints (ahimsa-nonviolence, satya-truth, asteya-non-stealing).
2. **Niyama:** Personal observances (saucha-purity, santosha-contentment, tapas-discipline).
3. **Asana:** Physical postures.
4. **Pranayama:** Breath control.
5. **Pratyahara:** Withdrawal of senses.
6. **Dharana:** Concentration.
7. **Dhyana:** Meditation.
8. **Samadhi:** Enlightenment/absorption.

## Evidence base

Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) and yoga have robust evidence for:
Reducing stress, anxiety, and depression. Chronic pain relief. Improving sleep quality.
Yoga for chronic low back pain: Strong evidence. Comparable to physical therapy.
