# Vietnamese Education System

## School structure

Tiểu học (Primary, 5 years) → THCS (Lower secondary, 4 years) → THPT (Upper secondary, 3 years) → University.

## THPT Quốc gia (National High School Graduation Exam)

Annual exam at end of grade 12. Used for both graduation and university admissions.

**Mandatory subjects (all students):**
Mathematics, Literature, Foreign Language (usually English).

**Choose 1 combination:**
Natural Sciences: Physics, Chemistry, Biology.
Social Sciences: History, Geography, Civic Education.
Each subject: 0-10 scale.

## University admissions (xét tuyển)

**Combination scoring:** Average of 3 subjects (called a "combination" or "khối").
Common combinations:
A00: Math, Physics, Chemistry (engineering)
B00: Math, Chemistry, Biology (medicine, pharmacy)
D01: Math, Literature, English (economics, business)

**Admission thresholds:** Published by each university. Competitive programs: 25-29/30.

## Top Vietnamese universities

**VNU-HCM (Vietnam National University HCMC):** Largest. Technical and social sciences.
**VNU Hanoi:** National flagship research university.
**HUST (Hanoi University of Science and Technology):** #1 engineering.
**NEU (National Economics University):** Economics and business.
**University of Medicine and Pharmacy HCM:** Top medical school.

## Vietnamese language (tiếng Việt)

6 tones (similar challenge to Mandarin). Latin-based alphabet (easier to read than Chinese).
**VSTEP:** Vietnamese Standardized Test of English Proficiency — for Vietnamese, not Vietnamese language.
Vietnamese language proficiency: No widely standardized international test.

Sources: Ministry of Education Vietnam (moet.gov.vn — free), VNU Vietnam (free)
