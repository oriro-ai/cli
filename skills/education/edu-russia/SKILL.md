# Russian Education System

## School structure

Nachal'naya shkola (Primary, grades 1-4) → Osnovnaya shkola (Basic, grades 5-9) →
Senior school (grades 10-11) or vocational college.

## EGE (Единый государственный экзамен / Unified State Examination)

National final exam in grades 10-11. Results used for university admissions.
**Mandatory subjects:** Russian Language, Mathematics (Basic or Profile level).
**Optional subjects:** Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Social Studies, Geography, Foreign Languages, Literature, Computer Science.
**Scale:** 0-100. University minimum thresholds published annually.

## Top Russian universities

**MSU (Lomonosov Moscow State University):** Oldest (1755), most prestigious. QS Top 100.
**MIPT (Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology):** "Russian MIT." Physics, mathematics, engineering.
**MEPhI:** Nuclear physics and engineering.
**HSE (Higher School of Economics):** Economics, social sciences.
**SPbU (Saint Petersburg State University):** Russia's second oldest (1724).

## University admissions

Public universities: Free for Russian citizens in government-funded spots.
Competitive subjects: Medicine, Law, International Relations, Computer Science.
EGE scores + olympiad results + portfolio for creative programs.

## TORFL (Test of Russian as a Foreign Language)

6 levels: Elementary, Basic, Certified (1), Certified (2), Advanced (3), Mastery (4).
B1 level = tourism and daily communication.
B2 = university study in Russia.
