# Mandarin Chinese and Chinese Education

## Tonal system (声调)

Mandarin has 4 tones + neutral tone:
1st tone (ˉ): High and level. mā (mother) — 妈.
2nd tone (ˊ): Rising. má (hemp/numb) — 麻.
3rd tone (ˇ): Dipping. mǎ (horse) — 马.
4th tone (ˋ): Falling sharply. mà (scold) — 骂.
Neutral tone: Short, unstressed. ma (question particle) — 吗.

## Pinyin romanization

Standard romanization. Used for input methods and learning.
Finals: a, o, e, i, u, ü and combinations.
Initials: b, p, m, f, d, t, n, l, g, k, h, j, q, x, zh, ch, sh, r, z, c, s.
Special: "j, q, x" only take "ü". "zh, ch, sh, r" = retroflex consonants.

## HSK (Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi) levels

China's official Chinese proficiency test.
HSK 1: 150 words. Basic survival.
HSK 2: 300 words. Simple daily conversation.
HSK 3: 600 words. Life and work basics.
HSK 4: 1,200 words. University admission minimum. Wide range of topics.
HSK 5: 2,500 words. Read newspapers, watch films.
HSK 6: 5,000+ words. Professional fluency.

## Gaokao (高考)

China's National College Entrance Exam. Most important exam in Chinese students' lives.
**Subjects:** Chinese (语文), Mathematics (数学), English (英语), plus 3 optional subjects.
**Admission:** Tsinghua, Peking University require top 0.01% nationally. Extremely competitive.

## Chinese education structure

Elementary: 6 years. Junior High: 3 years (compulsory ends here).
Senior High: 3 years. University: 4 years for bachelor's.
