# Child Health

## Developmental milestones

### 2 months

Social smile, begins to coo, can hold head up briefly. Tracks objects with eyes.
**Red flags:** Not smiling at people, not watching things as they move.

### 4 months

Laughs, holds head steady, pushes up on elbows during tummy time.
**Red flags:** Doesn't bring things to mouth, not pushing down with legs.

### 6 months

Begins to babble, sits with support, rolls both ways, starts solid foods.
**Red flags:** Not reaching for things, not showing affection, not laughing.

### 9 months

Stands holding on, crawling, says mama/dada nonspecifically, stranger anxiety.

### 12 months

First words, walks with support, waves bye-bye, points.
**Red flags:** No babbling, no pointing, no single words.

### 18 months

15+ words, walks well, copies others, points to body parts.
**Red flags:** Not walking, fewer than 6 words, not noticing others' distress.

### 24 months

50+ words, 2-word phrases, runs, kicks ball, follows 2-step directions.
**Red flags:** No 2-word phrases, not following instructions.

### 36 months

300+ words, 3-word sentences, climbs and runs, pretend play, parallel play with peers.

## Vaccination schedule (CDC recommended, US 2024)

**Birth:** Hepatitis B (#1)
**2 months:** DTaP, Hib, IPV, PCV15/20, RV, HepB (#2)
**4 months:** DTaP, Hib, IPV, PCV, RV
**6 months:** DTaP, Hib, PCV, RV, HepB (#3), Flu (annual starting here)
**12-15 months:** MMR, Varicella, Hib, PCV, HepA (#1)
**15-18 months:** DTaP
**18-23 months:** HepA (#2)
**4-6 years:** DTaP, MMR, Varicella, IPV
**11-12 years:** Tdap, HPV series (2 doses), MenACWY
**16 years:** MenACWY booster

## Common childhood illnesses

**Fever management:** Under 3 months with any fever → emergency care. 3-12 months > 102.2°F → call doctor. Over 1 year: treat comfort (acetaminophen, ibuprofen after 6 months). Fever itself is not dangerous (normal immune response); the underlying illness is what matters.
**DO NOT give aspirin to children** (Reye's syndrome risk).
Acetaminophen dosing by weight: 10-15 mg/kg every 4-6 hours.
Ibuprofen dosing by weight: 5-10 mg/kg every 6-8 hours.

**Ear infections:** Most resolve without antibiotics (watchful waiting 48-72 hours in mild cases for age 2+). Amoxicillin first-line when needed.
**Croup:** Barky seal cough, usually at night. Cool night air or steam often helps. Severe stridor at rest → emergency.
**RSV bronchiolitis:** Common cause of infant hospitalization in winter. Supportive care. Nirsevimab (Beyfortus) available for high-risk infants.
**Hand-foot-mouth disease:** Coxsackie virus. Vesicles in mouth and on hands/feet. Self-limiting. Treat symptoms.
**Febrile seizures:** Occur in 2-5% of children 6 months - 5 years. Alarming but usually benign. Protect child from injury. Call 911 if > 5 minutes.

## Growth monitoring

Weight, height, head circumference plotted on WHO/CDC growth charts at every visit.
Normal: consistent tracking along a percentile (any percentile is fine).
Concerning: crossing percentile lines downward over time.

## Child safety

**Car seat:** Rear-facing until age 2 or maximum weight for seat. Forward-facing until 4-7+. Booster until 8-12 or until seat belt fits properly.
**SIDS prevention:** Back to sleep (always), firm flat surface, no soft bedding, no smoking around baby, room-share (not bed-share) for at least 6 months.
**Choking hazards:** Round foods (grapes, hot dogs, nuts) must be cut into small pieces until age 4.
