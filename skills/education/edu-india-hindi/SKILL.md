# Hindi Language and CBSE Curriculum

## Hindi grammar (व्याकरण)

### Parts of speech (शब्द भेद)

**संज्ञा (Noun/Sangya):** नाम (Name). व्यक्तिवाचक (Proper), जातिवाचक (Common), भाववाचक (Abstract), समूहवाचक (Collective), द्रव्यवाचक (Material).
**सर्वनाम (Pronoun/Sarvnam):** Replaces noun. मैं, तुम, वह, यह, हम, आप, कोई, कुछ.
**विशेषण (Adjective/Visheshan):** Describes noun. गुणवाचक (Quality), परिमाणवाचक (Quantity), संख्यावाचक (Number), सार्वनामिक (Pronominal).
**क्रिया (Verb/Kriya):** Action. सकर्मक (Transitive), अकर्मक (Intransitive).
**क्रिया विशेषण (Adverb):** Modifies verb.
**संबंधबोधक (Preposition/Relation):** के, में, पर, से, के लिए.
**समुच्चयबोधक (Conjunction):** और, परंतु, क्योंकि, इसलिए.
**विस्मयादिबोधक (Interjection):** अरे!, वाह!, हाय!

### Tenses (काल)

**भूतकाल (Past tense):** वह गया (simple past), वह गया था (past perfect), वह जा रहा था (past continuous).
**वर्तमान काल (Present):** वह जाता है (simple), वह जा रहा है (continuous), वह गया है (perfect).
**भविष्यकाल (Future):** वह जाएगा, वह जाएगा (simple), वह जाता रहेगा (continuous).

### Sandhi (संधि) — joining of sounds

**स्वर संधि:** Two vowels join. रमा + ईश = रमेश (a + i → e). Rules: दीर्घ, गुण, वृद्धि, यण, अयादि.
**व्यंजन संधि:** Consonant + vowel/consonant join. षट् + आनन = षडानन.
**विसर्ग संधि:** Visarga changes. नि: + छल = निश्छल.

### Samaas (समास) — compound words

**अव्ययीभाव:** First word avyay (indeclinable). प्रतिदिन, यथाशक्ति.
**तत्पुरुष:** Second word dominant. राजमहल = राजा का महल.
**कर्मधारय:** Adjective-noun. नीलकमल = नीला कमल.
**द्विगु:** Number first. त्रिभुज = तीन भुजाओं का.
**द्वंद्व:** Both equal. राम-सीता, आना-जाना.
**बहुव्रीहि:** Neither dominant; refers to third thing. दशानन = रावण (ten-headed one).

## CBSE Board examination strategy

### Class 10 Board (Secondary)

**Core subjects:** Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, English, Hindi (or other language).
**Marking:** 80 marks written + 20 marks internal assessment.
**CBSE datesheet:** Usually Feb-March. Results April-May.
**Pass marks:** 33% in each subject.

**Mathematics strategy:**
NCERT exemplar problems: Do all before board exam.
Practice previous 10 years papers: Repeat questions appear every year.
Focus: Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry, Probability, Polynomials.

**Science strategy:**
Biology: Learn NCERT diagrams — labeled diagrams carry 3-5 marks.
Physics: Derivations and numericals. Electricity and Light are heavy chapters.
Chemistry: Reactions and equations. Carbon compounds important.

### Class 12 Board (Senior Secondary)

**Streams:** PCM (Science-Maths), PCB (Science-Biology), Commerce, Arts/Humanities.
**Core PCM:** Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English + one elective.
**Percentage:** College admissions based on Class 12 % in relevant subjects.
DU cutoffs: Science colleges 95-99% for top programs.

**Mathematics (Class 12):**
Relations and Functions, Inverse Trigonometry, Matrices and Determinants, Calculus (most marks — Derivatives + Integrals + Applications + Differential Equations), Vectors and 3D Geometry, Linear Programming, Probability.
NCERT + NCERT Exemplar + R.D. Sharma for practice.

**Physics (Class 12):**
Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects, EMI + AC, Optics, Dual Nature, Atoms + Nuclei, Semiconductors.
Most important: Draw neat diagrams. Derive formulas step by step. NCERT back exercises mandatory.

**Chemistry (Class 12):**
Solid State, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, Surface Chemistry, p-block elements, d-f block, Coordination Compounds, Biomolecules, Polymers, Organic reactions.
Inorganic + reactions for maximum marks. NCERT NCERT NCERT.

## Essay writing (निबंध लेखन)

**Structure:** प्रस्तावना (Introduction) → विषय विस्तार (Body, 3-4 paragraphs) → उपसंहार (Conclusion).
**Important topics:** प्रदूषण, बेरोजगारी, आधुनिकीकरण, सोशल मीडिया, महिला सशक्तिकरण, डिजिटल भारत, स्वच्छ भारत, मेरा भारत महान.
**Language:** Simple, flowing. Avoid direct translation from English. Use Hindi idioms (मुहावरे).
**Common मुहावरे:** कान भरना (to instigate), आँखें खुलना (to realize), नमक-मिर्च लगाना (to exaggerate).

## Hindi literature (साहित्य) — Class 9-12

**Kshitij (क्षितिज) — NCERT anthology:**
Class 9: कबीर, मीराबाई, रसखान, सुमित्रानंदन पंत, माखनलाल चतुर्वेदी.
Class 10: सूरदास, तुलसीदास, देव, जयशंकर प्रसाद, सुमित्रानंदन पंत, महादेवी वर्मा, सूर्यकांत त्रिपाठी निराला.

**For board preparation:**
Learn poet/author introductions.
Know literary context and themes of each poem/story.
Practice question-answer from NCERT guidelines.
Write explanation (भावार्थ) of poems in your own words.
