# French Education System

## Structure

Ecole maternelle (3-6) → Ecole primaire (6-11) → Collège (11-15) → Lycée (15-18) → Université/Grandes Ecoles.

## Baccalauréat

General Bac: 3 specialties chosen at age 16. Final exams at age 18.
**Filières:** Scientifique, Économique et Social, Littéraire.
**Grades:** 0-20. 10 = pass. 16+ = Bien. 18+ = Très Bien.

## Grandes Écoles

Elite institutions above standard universities.
**Classes Préparatoires (CPGE):** 2 years of intensive study post-Bac before Grandes Ecoles entry exam.
Taupe (math/physics): For Ecole Polytechnique, Centrale, Mines. Most demanding in France.
Khagne (literature): For Ecole Normale Supérieure.
HEC, Sciences Po, ESSEC: Business and political science elite schools.

## University system (LMD)

Licence (3 years) → Master (2 years) → Doctorat (3+ years).
**Parcoursup:** National university application platform.
Selective universities (IUT, BUT, BTS): More vocational, competitive entry.

## DALF/DELF (French language certification)

DELF: A1-B2 for general French. DALF: C1-C2 advanced.
Recognized globally for French language proficiency.

Sources: Education Nationale France (education.gouv.fr — free), Campus France (free guides)
