# Naturopathy and Ayurveda

## Ayurvedic medicine fundamentals

### Three doshas

**Vata (air + ether):** Movement, creativity, change. Imbalance: anxiety, dryness, constipation.
**Pitta (fire + water):** Digestion, transformation, intelligence. Imbalance: inflammation, anger.
**Kapha (earth + water):** Structure, stability, lubrication. Imbalance: lethargy, congestion.

### Six tastes (rasas)

Madhura (sweet), Amla (sour), Lavana (salty), Tikta (bitter), Katu (pungent), Kasaya (astringent).
All six should be present in each meal for balance.

### Agni (digestive fire)

Good agni = health. Weak/irregular agni = disease origin.
**Ama:** Undigested metabolic waste. Accumulation causes disease.

## Key Ayurvedic herbs (evidence-reviewed)

**Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera):** Most studied adaptogen. Reduces cortisol, improves stress resilience. Clinical evidence for anxiety reduction and athletic performance. Dosage: 300-600mg root extract daily.

**Turmeric/Curcumin:** Anti-inflammatory. Pair with black pepper (piperine increases absorption 20×). Clinical evidence for osteoarthritis.

**Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri):** Memory and cognitive function. Clinical evidence for children's attention and adult memory.

**Boswellia (Shallaki):** Anti-inflammatory. Clinical evidence for osteoarthritis and asthma.

**Triphala:** Three-fruit combination. Antioxidant, mild laxative, digestive tonic.

## Naturopathy principles

1. First do no harm.
2. The healing power of nature.
3. Identify and treat the cause — not just symptoms.
4. Doctor as teacher.
5. Treat the whole person.
6. Prevention.

## Evidence assessment

Well-evidenced: Ashwagandha, Boswellia, Bacopa, turmeric for specific conditions.
**Caution:** Heavy metals in some traditional Ayurvedic formulations — verify any supplement.
