# Automotive Maintenance and Repair

## Maintenance schedule (general — check your owner's manual)

**Every month/3,000 miles:**
Check engine oil level (dipstick — between min and max when cold).
Check tire pressure (placard on door jam = correct pressure; NOT tire sidewall max pressure).
Check other fluid levels: coolant (transparent reservoir), brake fluid, power steering, windshield washer.

**Every 5,000-7,500 miles or 6 months (modern cars):**
Oil and filter change. (Some modern cars go 10,000+ miles — check your manual.)
Tire rotation.

**Every 15,000-30,000 miles:**
Air filter (engine and cabin).
Tire balance and alignment check.
Inspect brakes.

**Every 30,000-60,000 miles:**
Spark plugs (iridium plugs: 60,000-100,000 miles).
Transmission fluid.
Coolant flush.
Drive belts inspection.
Battery test.

**Every 60,000-100,000 miles:**
Timing belt (if applicable — interference engine: critical, failure destroys engine).
Brake fluid flush.
Power steering fluid.
Differential fluid.

## Reading warning lights

**Red lights = STOP driving (or take immediate action):**
Oil pressure: Stop immediately. Low/no oil pressure → engine damage in minutes.
Temperature: Overheating. Stop and cool down.
Battery: Charging system failure. May have limited time.
Brake: Check level or brake problem.

**Yellow/orange lights = Service needed soon:**
Check engine: Many causes. Get codes read. Steady = manageable. Flashing = catalyst damage risk (reduce load/speed).
Low tire pressure (TPMS).
ABS: Anti-lock brakes disabled.

**Reading OBD-II codes:** Plug scanner (basic: $20-50) into port under dash. Reads P, U, B, C codes. Lookup code for likely causes.

## Tires

**Tire pressure:** Check monthly when cold (before driving). See door placard for correct PSI.
Under-inflated: Poor fuel economy, wear outer edges, handling risk.
Over-inflated: Wear center, harsh ride, blowout risk.

**Tire reading:** 225/55R17 = 225mm width / 55% aspect ratio / R = radial / 17" wheel.
**Tread depth:** Quarter test — insert penny head-down; if you see Lincoln's head above tread, replace.
1/16" (1.6mm) minimum. 2/32" = new tire minimum. Start shopping at 4/32".

**Alignment:** If car pulls to one side or tire wear is uneven, get alignment checked.
**Balance:** Vibration at highway speed. Check balance.

## Buying a car

**Private seller vs. dealer:** Private = cheaper; dealer = some protections, certified pre-owned warranty.
**Pre-purchase inspection:** Always pay a mechanic $100-150 to inspect before buying used vehicle.
**Vehicle history:** Carfax or AutoCheck. Check for: accidents, odometer rollback, flood damage, title issues.
**True cost of ownership:** Insurance, fuel, maintenance, depreciation, financing. Not just purchase price.
**Depreciation:** New cars lose 20-30% in first year. 3-year-old cars offer best value typically.
