# International Business

## Market entry strategies

**Direct export:** Sell from home country. Low investment. Limited market control.
**Licensing:** Allow foreign company to use IP for royalty. Low risk. Limited upside.
**Franchising:** More control than licensing. Operations replication. McDonald's model.
**Joint venture:** Shared ownership with local partner. Market knowledge + capital sharing. Risk = partner alignment.
**Wholly owned subsidiary:** Full control. Highest investment. Best for strategic markets.
**Greenfield vs acquisition:** Build from scratch vs buy existing business.

## Cross-cultural management

**Hofstede's dimensions:**
Power distance: Acceptance of hierarchical inequality.
Individualism vs collectivism: Personal vs group orientation.
Uncertainty avoidance: Tolerance for ambiguity.
Long-term vs short-term orientation.
Masculinity vs femininity: Competition vs nurturing values.

High context cultures (Japan, China, Arab): Context and relationships matter. Indirect.
Low context cultures (Germany, US, Australia): Explicit communication. Direct.

## Trade finance

**Letter of Credit (LC):** Bank guarantees payment upon document presentation. Most secure for exporters.
**Documentary Collection:** Less expensive than LC. More risk for exporter.
**Open account:** Ship goods, invoice later. Most risk for exporter. Common in established relationships.
**EXIM Bank:** Government export credit agency. Financing and insurance for exporters.

## International contracts

**Choice of law:** Which country's law governs disputes.
**ICC Arbitration:** International Chamber of Commerce arbitration. Common alternative to litigation.
**CISG:** UN Convention on International Sale of Goods. Applies automatically in 95+ countries.
**Force majeure:** Clause excusing performance due to unforeseen events.
