# Sales

## Consultative selling fundamentals

Move from "product pusher" to "trusted advisor."
Core shift: Stop talking about your product. Start understanding their problem.
The best sales call is 70% listening, 30% talking.

**SPIN Selling (Rackham):**
S — Situation questions: Understand current context. (Don't over-ask — research first.)
P — Problem questions: Surface explicit problems or dissatisfactions.
I — Implication questions: Help buyer understand consequences of the problem.
N — Need-payoff questions: Get buyer to articulate the value of a solution.
"What would it mean for your team if you could eliminate this problem?"

**The Challenger Sale (Dixon & Adamson):**
Top performers teach, tailor, and take control.
Teach the customer something new about their business.
Tailor the message to their specific concerns.
Take control of the sales process diplomatically.
Insight-led selling: Lead with a provocative insight → disrupt their thinking → demonstrate your value.

## Sales process

**Lead → Qualify → Discovery → Solution → Proposal → Negotiation → Close → Onboard**

**Qualification (MEDDIC/BANT):**
BANT: Budget (can they pay?), Authority (can they decide?), Need (is there a real problem?), Timeline (are they ready to buy?).
MEDDIC (enterprise): Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion.
Disqualify early — wasted time on unqualified deals kills sales teams.

**Discovery call:** Purpose is to understand their world, not pitch your product.
Key questions: Current situation? Desired situation? What's preventing them from getting there? What happens if they don't solve this? What have they tried?

## Pipeline management

**Opportunity stages:** Match to buyer's journey, not your selling process.
Typical stages: Prospecting → Qualified → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won/Lost

**Win rate by stage:** Track ruthlessly. If 30% win from Proposal → Close, and you have $1M in proposal stage, you have ~$300K in pipeline.

**Pipeline coverage:** Ratio of pipeline value to quota. Need 3-4× for 80%+ quota attainment.

**Deal velocity:** Average deal cycle length. Longer = higher carry cost. Focus on stuck deals.

## Objection handling

**Listen fully before responding.** Most objections are questions or requests for more information.

**Common objections and responses:**
"It's too expensive": "What would the value of solving [problem] be worth to you?" or explore ROI. If genuinely unaffordable, disqualify.
"We're happy with our current vendor": "What would make you consider a change?" or "When does your contract come up?" or "What do they do really well? What could be better?"
"We don't have budget": "Is this a priority? If we could structure payment differently, would that help?" or "When does your budget cycle reset?"
"I need to think about it": "Of course. What specific concerns would you like to think through?" (Get the real objection.)
"Send me information": Often a polite brush-off. "I'd rather have a conversation to make sure it's relevant to you. What specifically were you hoping to learn?"

## Outbound prospecting

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):** Define precisely. Company size, industry, tech stack, pain indicators. Don't prospect outside ICP.
**Prospecting sequence:** Multi-channel, spaced over 2-4 weeks. Email + LinkedIn + phone. 7-12 touches before giving up on a qualified prospect.
**Cold email formula:** Relevance (why this person, why now) → Problem → Value → CTA (specific, low-friction).
"I noticed [specific thing]. Companies like yours often struggle with [specific problem]. We helped [similar company] [specific result]. Worth a 20-minute call?"

**LinkedIn prospecting:** Connect with note → Message with value → Follow up. Quality over quantity.

## Closing

**Ask for the business.** Surprising how many salespeople avoid this.
"Based on everything we've discussed, does this make sense to move forward?"
"What would need to happen to start [by date]?"
"Are you ready to make this happen?"

**Trial close (test commitment):** "If we could resolve [concern], would you be ready to move forward?"

**Never discount without getting something in return.** "If we can get to that price, are you ready to sign today?"
