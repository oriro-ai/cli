# Brazilian Education and Portuguese Language

## ENEM (Exame Nacional do Ensino Médio)

Brazil's national high school exit exam. Used for most university admissions.
**4 sections:** Languages, Human Sciences, Natural Sciences, Mathematics.
Plus Writing (Redação): Dissertative-argumentative essay. Score 0-1000.
Total: 5 scores of 0-1000 (TRI-based, not raw).

## University admissions

### SISU (Sistema de Seleção Unificada)

Federal system using ENEM scores for federal university admissions.
Apply to 2 choices. Top ENEM scorers fill public university spots.
**Federal universities:** Free tuition. UFMG, UFRS, UnB, UFRJ, USP (state, not federal).

### FUVEST (University of São Paulo)

USP (Universidade de São Paulo) — #1 in Latin America.
2-phase exam: First phase (MCQ) + Second phase (discursive).
Medicine at USP: ~68/1000 score required. Extremely competitive.

### Private universities (PROUNI/FIES)

PROUNI: Government scholarship for private universities for low-income students.
FIES: Government student loans for private universities.

## Portuguese language (Brazilian)

Very different from European Portuguese in accent, vocabulary, and some grammar.
**CELPE-Bras:** Official Brazilian Portuguese proficiency test. Intermediate, Upper-intermediate, Advanced, Advanced Superior.
Required for university study and professional licenses in Brazil.
