# Hospitality Management

## Hotel operations structure

**Rooms division:** Front office (reservations, check-in, concierge), Housekeeping, Laundry.
**Food and beverage:** Restaurant(s), bar, room service, banquets/catering, mini-bar.
**Sales and marketing:** Group sales, corporate accounts, events.
**Revenue management:** Pricing optimization.
**Finance:** Accounting, purchasing, cost control.
**Engineering/Maintenance:** Building systems, preventive maintenance.
**Security.**

## Key performance metrics

### Revenue metrics

**ADR (Average Daily Rate):** Total room revenue / Rooms sold.
**Occupancy rate:** Rooms sold / Rooms available.
**RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room):** ADR × Occupancy. Or: Total room revenue / Total rooms available.
Most important single metric for hotel performance.
**TRevPAR (Total Revenue Per Available Room):** Includes all revenue (rooms + F&B + spa, etc.).
**GOPPAR (Gross Operating Profit Per Available Room):** Profitability after operating expenses.

### Cost metrics

**Cost per occupied room (CPOR):** Variable room costs / Occupied rooms.
**Labor cost %:** Labor / Revenue. Rooms: 30-35%. F&B: 30-35% of F&B revenue.
**Food cost % (F&B):** Food cost / Food revenue. Target: 28-32%.
**Beverage cost %:** Target: 18-24%.

## Revenue management

**Goal:** Sell the right room to the right customer at the right time for the right price.
**Dynamic pricing:** Adjust rates based on demand, booking window, competitor rates, events.
**STAR report (STR):** Competitive benchmarking. Compares your ADR/Occupancy/RevPAR to comp set. Indices > 100 = outperforming.
**Distribution channels:** Direct (best — no commission), OTAs (Booking.com, Expedia — 15-25% commission), GDS, wholesale.
**Rate parity:** Contractual obligation to maintain consistent rates across channels with many OTAs.

## Guest experience

**Touch points:** Pre-arrival (booking experience, pre-arrival email), arrival (check-in, first impression), during stay (room quality, F&B, requests), departure (check-out experience), post-stay (response to reviews, follow-up).
**NPS and satisfaction scores:** Internal targets + TripAdvisor/OTA ratings impact future bookings significantly. 10-point rating increase → ~5% increase in bookings.
**Personalization:** Recognize repeat guests. Note preferences. Small gestures (welcome amenity, room upgrade when available) drive loyalty disproportionately.
**Service recovery:** Recover problems quickly and generously. Guest who experienced a problem and had it resolved well is often more loyal than guest with no problem.

## Hotel brands and management structures

**Owner:** Owns the real estate.
**Operator/Manager:** Runs the hotel under a management contract. Fee: base (2-4% of revenue) + incentive.
**Franchisor/Brand:** Licenses brand standards, reservations system, loyalty program. Royalty: 3-7% of rooms revenue.
**Third-party management company:** Operates hotel for owner. Common when brand doesn't self-operate.
