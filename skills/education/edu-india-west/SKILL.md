# West Indian Education Systems

## Maharashtra

### MHT-CET (Maharashtra Common Entrance Test)

For engineering and pharmacy admissions in Maharashtra.
**Engineering:** PCM (Mathematics + Physics + Chemistry). Each 100 marks. Total 300.
**Score calculation:** 50% MHT-CET + 50% HSC (12th) board marks.
Top colleges: COEP Pune, VJTI Mumbai, SPCE Mumbai, DJ Sanghvi.
**IITs via JEE:** IIT Bombay is most sought-after IIT nationally.

### Maharashtra State Board (MSBSHSE)

Second largest state board in India. Mumbai exam culture — coaching heavy.
Pune: Major engineering education hub alongside Mumbai.

### CAP (Centralized Admission Process)

Online centralized admission. All MH colleges through single window.

## Gujarat

### GUJCET (Gujarat Common Entrance Test)

State-level engineering + pharmacy entrance exam.
**Subjects:** Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics. 40 marks each.
**Score:** GUJCET (40%) + Board HSC (60%).
Top institutions: LDCE Ahmedabad, SVNIT Surat, DDU Nadiad, PDPU.

### GSEB (Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board)

Strong focus on science education. Ahmedabad coaching ecosystem.

## Goa

### Goa CET (GCET)

Medical and engineering common entrance test for Goa institutions.
Goa Engineering College, NIT Goa, Goa College of Pharmacy.
Small state — limited seats.

## Rajasthan

### RPET (Rajasthan Pre Engineering Test) / JEE State Counselling

Engineering admissions in Rajasthan public colleges.
**Kota:** India's most famous coaching city. Allen, Resonance, Vibrant headquarters.
Thousands of students from across India come to Kota for JEE/NEET preparation.

## Common elements

MH and Gujarat states have large, well-funded private engineering colleges.
NEET mandatory for medical — no state exemptions since Supreme Court ruling.
Strong Gujarati and Marathi business communities support education investment.
