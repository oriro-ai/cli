# India Competitive Exam Preparation

## JEE (Joint Entrance Examination)

### JEE Main

**Conducting body:** NTA (National Testing Agency).
**For:** B.Tech/B.E. admissions at NITs, IIITs, CFTIs, and state engineering colleges.
**Pattern:** 90 questions — Physics (30), Chemistry (30), Mathematics (30). Total 300 marks.
Section A: 20 MCQs (4 marks each, -1 negative marking).
Section B: 10 numerical value questions (4 marks, no negative marking).
**Duration:** 3 hours. **Frequency:** Twice a year (January and April sessions).
**Cutoff for JEE Advanced:** General ~88-93 percentile (varies yearly).

### JEE Advanced

**For:** Admission to all 23 IITs.
**Eligibility:** Top 2.5 lakh rankers from JEE Main.
**Pattern:** Paper 1 and Paper 2 (3 hours each). Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics in each.
Question types: MCQ (single/multiple correct), numerical, paragraph-based, match-the-column.
Partial marking for multiple correct. Negative marking varies by question type.
**Difficulty:** Most competitive engineering exam in the world. Only ~4,000-5,000 clear for IIT CS.

### JEE syllabus overview

**Mathematics:** Algebra (complex numbers, matrices, quadratics, permutations), Calculus (limits, derivatives, integrals, differential equations), Coordinate geometry, Vector algebra, Probability, Trigonometry.
**Physics:** Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Waves, Optics, Electricity and Magnetism, Modern Physics.
**Chemistry:** Physical (atomic structure, chemical bonding, thermodynamics, kinetics, electrochemistry), Organic (reactions, mechanisms, named reactions), Inorganic (p-block, d-block elements, qualitative analysis).

### JEE preparation strategy

**Timeline:** Ideal 2 years (Class 11 + 12). Minimum 6 months crash course possible.
**Study resources (best, all free/affordable):**
NCERT textbooks (Class 11+12): Foundation for Chemistry. Essential for Physics concepts.
H.C. Verma "Concepts of Physics" (Vol 1&2): Best Physics for JEE.
I.E. Irodov "Problems in General Physics": Advanced practice (after basics strong).
FIITJEE/Allen/Resonance modules: Available as PDFs, comprehensive.
Previous years papers (1978-present): Public domain, free online.
**Daily schedule:** 8-10 hours study. Alternate subjects daily. Revise every week.
**Mock tests:** Full-length mocks every Sunday from 6 months before exam.

## NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test)

**For:** MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BUMS, BVSc admissions across India.
**Conducting body:** NTA.
**Pattern:** 200 questions (attempt 180). Biology (90 + 10 optional), Physics (45+5), Chemistry (45+5).
4 marks each. −1 negative marking.
**Duration:** 3 hours 20 minutes. **Once per year** (May).
**Marks:** 720 maximum. AIIMS Delhi: ~700+ marks needed. Government medical: ~600+ needed (varies by state/category).

### NEET syllabus

**Biology (50% of exam — most important):**
Botany: Plant kingdom, morphology, anatomy, reproduction, plant physiology, ecology.
Zoology: Animal kingdom, structural organization, human physiology, reproduction, genetics and evolution, biotechnology, ecology.
**Physics:** Mechanics, thermodynamics, optics, electrostatics, current electricity, dual nature, atoms and nuclei.
**Chemistry:** Physical, organic, inorganic chemistry (similar to JEE but easier level).

### NEET strategy

NCERT is the Bible for NEET. 90%+ questions directly from NCERT text.
Read NCERT Biology textbook (11+12) at least 5 times. Highlight every line.
NCERT Chemistry: Read thoroughly. NCERT Physics: Concepts + examples.
After NCERT: MTG Fingertips (objective NCERT), DC Pandey (Physics), VK Jaiswal (Chemistry Inorganic), MS Chauhan (Organic), Trueman Biology.
Mock tests: NTA official mock tests (free, nta.ac.in).

## UPSC Civil Services Examination (IAS/IPS/IFS)

**Three stages:** Prelims → Mains → Interview (Personality Test).
**Selection:** ~1,000 selections per year (IAS ~180, IPS ~150, remaining IFS and other services).

### Prelims

**Paper 1 (GS):** 100 MCQs, 200 marks, 2 hours. History, Geography, Polity, Economics, Environment, Science, Current Affairs.
**Paper 2 (CSAT):** 80 MCQs, 200 marks, qualifying (33% = 66 marks needed). Reading comprehension, reasoning, basic maths.
**Negative marking:** 1/3 mark deducted.
**Cutoff:** ~93-98 marks (General category) in GS Paper 1.

### Mains

**9 papers total:**
Paper A (Indian Language): 300 marks, qualifying.
Paper B (English): 300 marks, qualifying.
Paper 1 (Essay): 250 marks.
Papers 2-5 (GS 1-4): 250 marks each.
GS 1: Indian Heritage + Culture + History + Geography.
GS 2: Governance + Constitution + Polity + Social Justice + International Relations.
GS 3: Technology + Economic Development + Bio-diversity + Environment + Security.
GS 4: Ethics + Integrity + Aptitude.
Papers 6-7 (Optional): 250 marks each (choose one subject from 25 options).

### UPSC preparation strategy

**Timeline:** 12-24 months minimum. Average successful candidate takes 2-3 attempts.
**Foundation resources:**
NCERT textbooks (6th to 12th): All subjects. Read twice.
The Hindu / Indian Express: Daily newspaper. Take notes.
Laxmikanth "Indian Polity": Most important book for polity.
Ramesh Singh "Indian Economy": Standard economic reference.
Spectrum Modern India History: Standard book for modern history.
NCERT Old + New Geography (Physical + Human).
**Current affairs:** Monthly magazines (Vision IAS, Insights IAS — free PDFs), Yojana, Kurukshetra (GoI publications, free).

## CAT (Common Admission Test) — MBA

**For:** IIM A/B/C/L/K/I and 100+ other top B-schools.
**Pattern:** 66 questions, 2 hours 40 minutes.
VARC (Verbal Ability + Reading Comprehension): 24 questions, 40 minutes.
DILR (Data Interpretation + Logical Reasoning): 20 questions, 40 minutes.
QA (Quantitative Aptitude): 22 questions, 40 minutes.
**Scoring:** Scaled score 0-300 for each section. Percentile-based selection.
IIM A/B/C: 99.5+ percentile needed for call.

### CAT preparation

**QA:** Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Number Systems, Modern Math. Arun Sharma / CAT by IIM graduates.
**VARC:** Heavy reading practice. Hindu editorial, 3-4 RC passages daily. Grammar from Wren and Martin.
**DILR:** Practice sets daily. Speed and accuracy. DILR 50 sets book.
**Mock tests:** Full-length mocks from August onwards. Analyze every mock thoroughly.

## GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering)

**For:** M.Tech admissions to IITs/NITs. PSU recruitment (BHEL, NTPC, GAIL, HPCL, etc.).
**29 papers:** CS, EC, EE, ME, CE, CH, BT, and many more.
**Pattern:** 65 questions, 100 marks, 3 hours.
General Aptitude: 10 questions (15 marks). Core subject: 55 questions (85 marks).
MCQ + Numerical Answer Type (NAT) — no negative marking for NAT.
**Score validity:** 3 years. Normalized score used for PSU cutoffs.

### SSC CGL / IBPS (briefly)

**SSC CGL:** Staff Selection Commission. Group B + C posts in central government.
4 tiers: Tier 1 (Prelims, MCQ), Tier 2 (Mains, MCQ), Tier 3 (Descriptive), Tier 4 (Skill test).
**IBPS PO/Clerk:** Banking probationary officer and clerk posts.
3 stages: Prelims → Mains → Interview.
Sections: English, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge.
