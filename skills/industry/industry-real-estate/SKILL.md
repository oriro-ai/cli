# Real Estate Industry

## The development process

1. **Site selection:** Feasibility analysis. Demographics, traffic, competition, zoning.
2. **Due diligence:** Title search, environmental (Phase I/II), survey, geotechnical, building inspection.
3. **Entitlement:** Zoning approvals, variances, permits. Often most time-consuming stage (months to years).
4. **Financing:** Construction loan (typically 65-75% LTC), equity raise, permanent financing commitment.
5. **Construction:** GC selection (bid or negotiate), subcontractors, draws against construction loan.
6. **Lease-up/Sale:** Marketing, broker relationships, lease negotiation.
7. **Stabilization/Exit:** Reaching target occupancy (typically 90-95%). Refinance or sale.

## Commercial real estate asset classes

**Office:** Urban/suburban, Class A/B/C. Vacancy elevated post-COVID. Work-from-home structural headwind.
**Industrial/Logistics:** Warehouses, distribution centers, manufacturing. Strongest sector — e-commerce demand.
**Retail:** Enclosed malls declining. Power centers, grocery-anchored centers more resilient. "Experiential" retail growing.
**Multifamily:** Apartments. Recession-resilient. Rising rents in supply-constrained markets.
**Hospitality:** Hotels. Highly cyclical. Brand affiliation and revenue management critical.
**Self-storage:** Counter-cyclical, recession-resilient.
**Data centers:** Fastest-growing sector. AI demand driving. Power and cooling intensive.
**Life sciences:** Labs and research facilities. High-barrier supply; strong demand.

## Key metrics

**NOI (Net Operating Income):** Revenue - Operating Expenses (not debt service). Core income metric.
**Cap rate:** NOI / Value. See finance-real-estate skill.
**Gross Lease:** Landlord pays most operating expenses. Tenant pays base rent only.
**NNN (Triple Net Lease):** Tenant pays rent + real estate taxes + insurance + maintenance. Landlord has minimal expenses.
**WALT (Weighted Average Lease Term):** Remaining lease duration weighted by rent. Longer = more valuable to investors.
**Vacancy rate vs. absorption:** Market-level metrics. Positive absorption = tenants taking more space than vacating.

## Brokerage and transactions

**Listing broker (seller's agent) vs. Buyer's broker:** Fiduciary duty to respective clients.
**CAP rate vs. GRM:** Different approaches to valuing the same income property.
**Letter of Intent (LOI):** Non-binding expression of intent. Establishes key terms before drafting purchase agreement.
**Purchase and Sale Agreement (PSA):** Binding contract. Includes: price, deposit, due diligence period, contingencies.
**1031 Exchange:** Tax deferral on investment property sale. Must identify replacement within 45 days, close within 180.
