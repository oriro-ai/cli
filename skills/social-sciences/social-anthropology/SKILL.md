# Cultural Anthropology

## Anthropology's four fields

**Cultural anthropology:** Human cultures, beliefs, practices, social organization.
**Biological/Physical anthropology:** Human evolution, variation, primatology.
**Archaeology:** Past human cultures through material remains.
**Linguistic anthropology:** Language and culture interaction.

## Core concepts

### Culture

Learned, shared, and dynamic system of meanings, values, norms, and practices.
Culture is not genetic — learned through socialization.
**Cultural relativism:** Understand practices within their cultural context before judging. Analytical stance, not moral relativism.
**Ethnocentrism:** Judging other cultures by your own culture's standards. Major source of misunderstanding and prejudice.

### Ethnography

Deep, immersive study of a culture or community.
**Participant observation:** Live within the community, participate in daily life while observing.
**Fieldwork:** Extended period (months to years). Learn the language. Build relationships.
**Thick description (Geertz):** Interpret behavior in layers of cultural context, not just describe actions.

### Kinship and family

Kinship systems organize societies in ways beyond Western nuclear family.
**Descent systems:**
Patrilineal: Descent through father's line (most common globally).
Matrilineal: Descent through mother's line (Hopi, Ashanti, and others).
Bilateral: Both sides (Western norm).
**Marriage patterns:** Monogamy, polygyny (1 man, multiple women), polyandry (1 woman, multiple men — rare), group marriage. All have cultural logic.

### Ritual and symbolism

Rituals mark transitions (rites of passage: birth, coming of age, marriage, death) and reinforce social bonds.
**Rites of passage (van Gennep):** Separation → Liminality (threshold, in-between) → Reincorporation.
Symbols condense complex cultural meanings. Same symbol can mean different things across cultures.

## Applied anthropology

Using anthropological methods and insights for practical problem-solving.
**Medical anthropology:** How culture affects health behavior, illness experience, and healthcare delivery.
**Development anthropology:** Ensure development projects fit local cultural context (top-down projects often fail without this).
**Business anthropology/UX research:** Ethnographic methods for product research and design.
**Forensic anthropology:** Identifying human remains for legal/investigative purposes.
