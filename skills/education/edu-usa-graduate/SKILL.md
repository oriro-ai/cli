# Graduate Admissions Tests

## GRE (Graduate Record Examination)

**Format:** 3 sections.
Verbal Reasoning: 40 questions, 60 minutes. Score 130-170.
Quantitative Reasoning: 40 questions. Score 130-170.
Analytical Writing: 2 essays, 60 minutes. Score 0-6.

**GRE Verbal:** Vocabulary critical. Text Completion, Sentence Equivalence, Reading Comprehension.
Build vocab: Barron's 3500 wordlist, Magoosh GRE flashcards (free).
**GRE Quant:** High school + early college math. Focus on speed and accuracy.
**Score targets by field:**
Top CS programs: Q 165+ recommended.
Top PhD programs: Q 165+, V 155+, AW 4.0+.
ETS free practice: 2 full-length official tests at ets.org.

## GMAT Focus Edition (2024)

**3 sections:**
Quantitative Reasoning: 21 questions, 45 minutes. Score 60-90.
Verbal Reasoning: 23 questions, 45 minutes. Score 60-90.
Data Insights: 20 questions, 45 minutes. Score 60-90.
Total: 205-805.
**Target:** 700+ (90th percentile) for top 10 programs. 730+ for M7.

## LSAT (Law School Admissions Test)

**4 scored sections:** Logical Reasoning (2), Analytical Reasoning (Logic Games), Reading Comprehension.
Score: 120-180. Top law schools: 170+.
Khan Academy LSAT Prep: Free, official partnership with LSAC.

## MCAT (Medical College Admissions Test)

**4 sections:** Biological/Biochemical Foundations, Chemical/Physical Foundations,
Psychological/Social/Biological Foundations, CARS (Critical Analysis).
Score: 472-528. Average for accepted MD students: ~511.5.
**Khan Academy MCAT:** Free, comprehensive. 1,100+ videos, 3,000+ practice questions.

## IELTS/TOEFL

IELTS 6.5 = standard for most masters programs. IELTS 7.0+ for competitive schools.
TOEFL iBT: 0-120 total. Most US schools require 80-100+.
