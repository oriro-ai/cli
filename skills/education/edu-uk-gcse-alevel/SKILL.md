# UK GCSE and A-Level Education

## GCSE (General Certificate of Secondary Education)

Students sit GCSE exams at age 16 (Year 11). Usually 8-10 subjects.
**Key subjects:** English Language, English Literature, Mathematics, Sciences,
plus humanities, languages, and options.
**Grades:** 9-1 (9 = highest). Grade 5+ = "good pass". Grade 4+ = "standard pass".

**Mathematics GCSE:**
Foundation tier: Grades 1-5. Higher tier: Grades 4-9.
Topics: Number, Algebra, Ratio, Geometry, Probability, Statistics.
BBC Bitesize: Free, excellent GCSE resources for all subjects.
Past papers: Available free from AQA, OCR, Edexcel websites.

## A-Level (Advanced Level)

Studied ages 16-18 (Years 12-13). Usually 3 A-level subjects.
Standard: A* A A = top offers. A A B = competitive offers.
**UCAS points:** A* = 56, A = 48, B = 40, C = 32, D = 24, E = 16.

**Mathematics A-Level:**
Pure (Year 1): Algebra, Functions, Trigonometry, Calculus, Exponentials.
Pure (Year 2): More calculus, integration by parts, parametric equations.
Statistics: Probability, distributions, hypothesis testing.
Mechanics: Kinematics, Forces, Newton's laws.

**A-Level strategy:**
Start past papers from beginning of Year 12.
Mark schemes are public domain — study them for examiner expectations.

## UCAS University Application

**Deadlines:** Main deadline January 31. Oxford/Cambridge/Medicine: October 15.
**Personal statement:** 4,000 characters. Why this subject. Evidence of passion.
**Offers:** Conditional (based on A-level results) or unconditional.
