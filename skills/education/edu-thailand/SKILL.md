# Thai Education System

## School structure

Prathom (Primary, P1-P6) → Matthayom (Secondary, M1-M3 lower, M4-M6 upper) → University.

## University admission — TCAS (Thai University Central Admission System)

**5 rounds:**
Round 1: Portfolio/talent-based (no exam). October-February.
Round 2: Quota (regional quotas, special quotas). Not all universities.
Round 3: Combined admission — uses A-Level scores.
Round 4: Direct admission by universities.
Round 5: Clearing round.

## A-Level (Academic Level)

New national test since 2021. Replaced PAT and O-NET for university admissions.
**Core subjects (all):** Thai Language, Social Studies, English.
**Optional subjects:** Mathematics 1 or 2, Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Additional Language, French, German, Japanese, Chinese, Korean.

## ONET (Ordinary National Educational Test)

End-of-secondary standardized test. Less weight in admissions now.

## Top Thai universities

**Chulalongkorn University (CU):** Most prestigious. QS Top 800 globally. Bangkok.
**Mahidol University:** Medical and health sciences. Research-focused.
**Kasetsart University:** Agriculture, engineering.
**KMUTT:** Engineering and technology.
**Thammasat University:** Law, political science, economics.

## Score requirements

Medicine at Chulalongkorn: Very competitive. Top 1% scores.
Engineering at CU or Mahidol: Top 10%.
Business programs: Top 20-30%.
