# Pregnancy and Maternal Health

## Prenatal care schedule (standard)

First trimester (0-13 weeks): First visit 8-10 weeks. Initial bloodwork, ultrasound, genetic screening options.
Second trimester (14-27 weeks): Visits every 4 weeks. Anatomy ultrasound at 18-20 weeks.
Third trimester (28-40+ weeks): Every 2 weeks until 36 weeks, then weekly.

## Key prenatal tests

**Cell-free DNA (NIPT) at 10+ weeks:** Screens for chromosomal abnormalities (Down syndrome, trisomies 18/13, sex chromosome). Non-invasive blood test. 99%+ sensitivity.
**First trimester screening (nuchal translucency + bloodwork):** Combined risk assessment.
**Anatomy scan at 18-20 weeks:** Detailed ultrasound of all organs, placenta, amniotic fluid.
**Glucose tolerance test at 24-28 weeks:** Screens for gestational diabetes.
**Group B Strep swab at 35-37 weeks:** Determines if antibiotics needed in labor.

## Nutrition in pregnancy

**Folic acid:** 400-800 mcg/day starting before conception and through first trimester. Prevents neural tube defects. Most prenatal vitamins contain 600-800 mcg.
**Iron:** 27 mg/day (prenatal vitamins contain this). Take with vitamin C for absorption. Constipation common side effect — increase fiber and water.
**DHA omega-3:** 200 mg/day minimum. Supports brain development. Prenatal with DHA or fish oil supplement.
**Calcium:** 1,000 mg/day.
**Iodine:** 150 mcg/day (many prenatal vitamins don't include — check label).
**Calorie addition:** ~0 extra in first trimester, ~340 in second, ~450 in third.

## Foods to avoid

**Raw/undercooked meat, fish, poultry:** Listeria, Toxoplasma, Salmonella risk.
**High-mercury fish:** Shark, swordfish, king mackerel, tilefish. Tuna: limit to 6 oz/week (canned light is lower mercury than albacore).
**Unpasteurized dairy and juice:** Listeria.
**Raw sprouts:** Salmonella and E. coli.
**Deli meats and hot dogs:** Unless heated to steaming (Listeria).
**Alcohol:** No safe amount established. Avoid completely.
**Caffeine:** Limit to 200 mg/day (one 12oz coffee).

## Warning signs requiring immediate medical contact

- Vaginal bleeding
- Severe abdominal pain or cramping
- Preeclampsia symptoms: severe headache, visual changes, swelling of face/hands, upper abdominal pain + nausea (seek emergency care)
- Reduced or absent fetal movement after 28 weeks (do kick counts: 10 movements in 2 hours)
- Leaking fluid (possible membrane rupture)
- Signs of preterm labor before 37 weeks: contractions < 10 min apart, pelvic pressure, low backache

## Labor stages

**Stage 1 (latent then active labor):** Cervix dilates from 0 to 10 cm. Latent (0-6cm): longest, can labor at home. Active (6-10cm): go to hospital when contractions 5-1-1 (5 min apart, 1 min long, 1 hour pattern).
**Stage 2 (pushing):** Crowning to delivery. 20 min to 3+ hours.
**Stage 3:** Delivery of placenta. 5-30 minutes.

## Postpartum

**Baby blues:** Mood swings, tearfulness first 2 weeks — normal; from hormone shifts.
**Postpartum depression:** Persistent (beyond 2 weeks) depression, anxiety, or inability to care for baby. Affects 10-15% of mothers. Highly treatable. Screen at 1-month and 3-month visits.
**Postpartum anxiety:** Equal or more common than PPD. Excessive worry, panic, intrusive thoughts.
**Physical recovery:** Vaginal birth: 6 week recovery. C-section: 8-12 week recovery (major surgery). No sex, tampons, or heavy lifting until cleared (6 weeks typical).
