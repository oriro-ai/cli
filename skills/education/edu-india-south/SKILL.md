# South Indian Education Systems

## Karnataka

### KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test)

State-level exam for Engineering, Architecture, B.Pharma, B.VSc.
**Subjects:** Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology (optional for Pharma/VSc).
Plus 12th board marks (SSLC/PUC) — 50% combined with KCET for ranking.
Top colleges: NITK Surathkal, BMS College, RVCE, MS Ramaiah (private).

### Class 12 boards

**PUC (Pre-University Course):** Karnataka's own board. Science/Commerce/Arts streams.
**CBSE and ICSE** also followed by many schools.

## Andhra Pradesh

### AP EAMCET (Engineering, Agriculture, Medical Common Entrance Test)

Engineering: PCM. Agriculture/Medical: PCB.
**Rank calculation:** 75% EAMCET + 25% qualifying exam marks.
Top colleges: NIT Warangal (JEE), JNTU Hyderabad, Andhra University.

## Telangana

### TS EAMCET

Separate from AP since bifurcation. Same format.
Top colleges: IIIT Hyderabad, BITS Hyderabad (private), NIT Warangal.

## Kerala

### KEAM (Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical)

Commissioner for Entrance Examinations.
**Engineering:** PCM. Score + qualifying exam.
Top institutions: CET Trivandrum, NIT Calicut, College of Engineering Thrissur.

## Tamil Nadu

### TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions)

Merit-based ONLY — no entrance exam. Based purely on 12th board percentage.
Unique in India: Equal weightage to Maths, Physics, Chemistry.
Top colleges: Anna University CEG, NIT Trichy, PSG College of Technology.

## Common features

All South Indian states use JEE for NIT/IIIT admissions alongside state quotas.
NEET mandatory for medical admissions across all states.
